Lab Boards privacy

Updated 16 October 2026

Internal lab workspace

Lab Boards is a private workspace for registered Braintime Lab members. Existing lab accounts are verified through Supabase. Notes, images, schedules and meeting records are stored on BraintimeDisk, the lab’s private server.

Member identities and roles control access. The browser uses a session cookie to stay signed in.

Google Drive connection

The owner may connect the dedicated lab Google account. Lab Boards uses Drive access to verify that account, copy selected Google Slides presentations, and store original uploaded PDFs, final whiteboard images and meeting records in a private archive folder.

The connection requests Drive permission so it can copy presentations shared with the lab account. The application writes archive copies to its configured folder; it does not alter source presentations or their sharing permissions.

Google connection credentials are stored privately on the lab server. Access tokens are not returned to members. Lab Boards does not use Google Drive data for advertising or to train AI models.

Archive access and retention

For Lab Meetings, active members can view materials from the latest completed meeting and the current or next scheduled meeting. Hosts and presenters can prepare their upcoming meeting materials. The owner can view all meetings, restrict any meeting to the owner or selected members, and grant selected members access to older archives. Other meeting archives remain owner-only by default. These controls do not add Google Drive sharing permissions.

New archive versions preserve earlier copies. Revoking Google access stops future archiving, but does not delete copies already stored on Drive or BraintimeDisk. Retained copies remain until the owner removes them.

Contact

For questions, access changes or removal of retained copies, contact Jihwan Myung at jihwan@tmu.edu.tw.
