How expression is calculated

Ct values are averaged within each sample and gene. ΔCt is the gene’s mean Ct minus the equal-weight mean of its reference-gene means. ΔΔCt compares this value with the mean ΔCt of the selected calibrators. Relative expression is 2−ΔΔCt, which assumes equal amplification efficiency near 100%.

A missing selected reference excludes that sample from normalization. Missing calibrator data are reported explicitly. Use the standard-curve tool to inspect assay efficiency.

Methods & references

Relative expression uses 2−ΔΔCt under the equal, near-100% efficiency assumption. Equal weighting of reference-gene mean Ct values corresponds to geometric averaging on the expression scale. Standard curves fit Ct = slope × log₁₀(quantity) + intercept; estimated efficiency is (10−1/slope − 1) × 100%. Replicate wells describe technical variation.
